# Neo-brutalist portfolio (design-5005)

## Theme

- **Palette:** `#0A0A0A` black, `#C8F135` lime accent, `#FFE500` yellow highlights, `#F4F0E8` paper background.
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk (display, uppercase headings), IBM Plex Sans (body).
- **Structure:** 4px black borders, hard offset shadows (`6px 6px 0`), no soft gradients.
- **Layout:** Asymmetric 12-column grids — hero split 8/4, experience cards offset, featured project spans 2 cols.
- **Tone:** Professional copy from `information.txt` + resume PDF; emphasis on shipped systems and production AI.
